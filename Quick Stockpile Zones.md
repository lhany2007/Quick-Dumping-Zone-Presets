# Quick Stockpile Zones

A small quality-of-life mod for players who find even setting up stockpile presets too bothersome.

**Quick Stockpile Zones** adds a right-click menu to the vanilla **Dumping stockpile zone** designator, allowing you to instantly place pre-configured stockpile zones without opening the filter tab.

No more manually configuring the same stockpile filters over and over.

## Features

- Right-click the vanilla **Dumping stockpile zone** designator to open the quick placement menu.
- Instantly place **6 pre-configured stockpile zones**.
- No need to open the filter tab.
- Stockpile priority is automatically configured.
- Uses the vanilla stockpile system and UI.

## Included Zones

| Zone | Allowed Items | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| **Chunk Zone** | Stone chunks, steel slag chunks, and mechanoid chunks | Important |
| **Toxic Wastepack Zone** | Toxic wastepacks only | Important |
| **Corpse Zone** | All corpses | Important |
| **Mechanoid Corpse Zone** | Mechanoid and drone corpses | Important |
| **Structure Zone** | All minified/packaged structures (entire Buildings category) | Important |
| **Butcher Zone** | Animals only, excluding rotten corpses | **Critical** |

## How to Use

1. Select the vanilla **Dumping stockpile zone** designator.
2. **Right-click** the designator.
3. Select the stockpile preset you want.
4. Place the zone.

That's it.

## Compatibility

Designed to work with the vanilla stockpile system.

The mod does not replace the vanilla stockpile zone or add a separate stockpile system. It simply provides quick presets through the existing **Dumping stockpile zone** designator.
